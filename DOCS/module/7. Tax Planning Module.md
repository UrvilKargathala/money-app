# 7. Tax Planning Module

**Module Type:** Optimization  
**Build Phase:** Phase 2  
**Dependencies:** Module 1 (Account & Wallet), Module 2 (Transaction Engine)

---

## Purpose

Indian tax optimization assistant that helps users track tax-saving investments, calculate liability under both old and new tax regimes, manage ITR document checklists, and generate actionable tax-saving plans. The module is designed around Indian tax structures — Section 80C, 80D, HRA, and other deduction sections — and targets salaried professionals, freelancers, and early-career investors.

## Functional Requirements

### Section Investment Tracking

**FR-7.1:** User can record tax-saving investments under the following sections:
- Section 80C: PPF, ELSS, LIC Premium, EPF (employee contribution), NSC, Home Loan Principal Repayment, Tuition Fees, Tax-Saving FDs (5-year lock-in), Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- Section 80D: Health Insurance Premium (self, family, parents)
- Section 80CCD(1B): NPS Tier-1 additional contribution (₹50,000 over and above 80C)
- Section 80E: Education Loan Interest
- Section 80G: Donations
- Section 80TTA / 80TTB: Savings Account Interest
- House Rent Allowance (HRA Exemption)
- Leave Travel Allowance (LTA Exemption)
- Standard Deduction

**FR-7.2:** Each investment entry captures:
- Section (required, e.g., "80C", "80D", "80CCD(1B)")
- Investment Name (required, e.g., "PPF - SBI", "LIC Jeevan Anand")
- Amount (required, positive decimal)
- Investment Date (required)
- Proof Status (required, enum: pending / collected / submitted / verified)
- Financial Year (required, format "2026-27")
- Linked Transaction ID (optional, to reference an existing expense transaction)
- Notes (optional)

**FR-7.3:** User can edit or delete any investment entry. Deleting removes it from the section utilization calculation.

**FR-7.4:** The system enforces no hard limit on entries per section. The user can add as many investments as they want under each section.

### Section Utilization Dashboard

**FR-7.5:** For each financial year, the dashboard shows every tax section with:
- Section name and description
- Maximum allowable limit (e.g., ₹1,50,000 for 80C)
- Total amount invested/claimed to date
- Remaining headroom (limit - invested)
- Utilization percentage with a progress bar
- Color coding: Green (< 80%), Yellow (80-95%), Orange (95-100%), Red (> 100% — warning that excess may not be deductible)

**FR-7.6:** Sections with zero investment are shown as empty rows with a "Not Started" label.

**FR-7.7:** A summary card at the top shows:
- Total amount invested across all sections
- Total maximum deduction available
- Estimated tax saved (at user's estimated slab rate, configurable)

### Old vs New Regime Comparison

**FR-7.8:** User can enter salary structure details:
- Monthly Basic Pay
- HRA (House Rent Allowance)
- LTA (Leave Travel Allowance)
- Special Allowances
- Employer PF Contribution
- Actual Rent Paid (for HRA exemption calculation)
- Other Exemptions (meal card, gift vouchers, etc.)

**FR-7.9:** For freelancers, user can enter gross annual income instead of salary structure.

**FR-7.10:** The system calculates and displays:
- **Old Regime:** Taxable income after deducting all declared 80C/80D/HRA/exemption investments
- **New Regime:** Taxable income under new tax regime (no deductions, lower slab rates)
- **Tax Payable (Old):** Calculated using old regime slab rates + cess
- **Tax Payable (New):** Calculated using new regime slab rates + cess
- **Recommended Regime:** Shows which regime results in lower tax, with the ₹ difference

**FR-7.11:** Tax slab rates are configured as reference data and can be updated when the government announces new budgets. Default slabs for FY 2026-27 are pre-seeded.

### ITR Document Checklist

**FR-7.12:** User can manage an ITR document checklist per financial year. Each document entry includes:
- Document Name (e.g., "Form 16", "Bank Statement", "PPF Statement", "ELSS Statement")
- Category (income_proof / investment_proof / deduction_proof / other)
- Status (pending / collected / submitted)
- Notes (optional)

**FR-7.13:** The document checklist shows a completion pie chart: pending vs collected vs submitted.

**FR-7.14:** Built-in document suggestions per financial year:
- Form 16 (mandatory for salaried users)
- Bank Account Statement (last 12 months)
- PPF Statement
- ELSS / Mutual Fund Statements
- LIC Premium Receipts
- NPS Statement
- Health Insurance Premium Receipts
- Home Loan Interest Certificate (Section 24)
- Rent Receipts / Rent Agreement (for HRA)
- Donation Receipts (Section 80G)

### Tax Saving Suggestions

**FR-7.15:** Based on the user's investments and unused section limits, the system shows plain-text suggestions:
- "You have ₹45,000 remaining under Section 80C. Consider investing in ELSS (3-year lock-in) or PPF (15-year lock-in)."
- "You have not claimed any deduction under Section 80D. Health insurance premiums up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens) are deductible."

### Notifications

**FR-7.16:** Notifications include:
- **Section limit approaching:** When any section reaches 90% utilization, a notification suggests completing the remaining investment
- **Filing deadline reminder:** Quarter 3 reminder (Dec), Quarter 4 reminder (Jan-Mar), and last-week reminder before July 31 ITR deadline
- **New financial year:** On April 1, a notification reminds the user to set up tax planning for the new FY

### Export

**FR-7.17:** Export section utilization summary as CSV: Section, Limit, Invested, Remaining, Utilization%.

**FR-7.18:** Export regime comparison as PDF showing: salary structure, deductions, taxable income (both regimes), tax payable (both regimes), recommended regime.

**FR-7.19:** Export ITR document checklist as CSV: Category, Document Name, Status.

### Tax Data Filters

**FR-7.20:** The tax module header has an explicit Financial-Year selector (formalizes F8's FY-by-FY navigation). Selecting a year re-renders section utilization, tax investments, and the ITR checklist for that FY. The selector auto-advances on April 1 (per FR-7.16).

**FR-7.21:** The tax-investments list can be filtered by section (multi-select chips from `tax_sections`, e.g., 80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B)). Filtering is applied client-side on the loaded list.

**FR-7.22:** The tax-investments list can be filtered by proof status: pending / collected / submitted / verified. No search box is needed at ~400–600 rows lifetime per user.

## Data Tables

### Table: tax_sections (Reference/Lookup)

Seeded reference data listing all supported tax deduction sections with their maximum limits.

| Column | Type | Constraints | Index | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|-------|-------------|
| section_code | TEXT | PK | PRIMARY KEY | Section code (e.g., "80C", "80D", "80CCD_1B") |
| section_name | TEXT | NOT NULL | | Display name (e.g., "Section 80C") |
| description | TEXT | NOT NULL | | Short description (e.g., "Investments & Payments") |
| max_limit | NUMERIC(12,2) | NOT NULL | | Maximum deductible amount for this section |
| applicable_regime | TEXT | NOT NULL, CHECK(applicable_regime IN ('old_only','both')) | | Which regime this section applies to |
| sort_order | INTEGER | NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 | | Display order |
| is_system | INTEGER | NOT NULL DEFAULT 1 | | System lookup; rows are seeded, not user-owned |

**Unique constraint:** `UNIQUE (section_code)` — system lookup, no user_id.

**Performance Notes:**
- This is a lookup table with ~15-20 rows. Never grows beyond that. All queries are < 0.1ms.
- Seeded at app initialization from built-in tax data (per `../data-tables-v2.md` § Module 7).

## Database Indexing Strategy

### Index Creation SQL

```sql
-- Tax Sections (lookup, no user_id)
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX idx_ts_code ON tax_sections(section_code);

-- Tax Investments (multi-user aware)
CREATE INDEX idx_ti_section_fy ON tax_investments(user_id, section_id, financial_year);
CREATE INDEX idx_ti_fy_section ON tax_investments(user_id, financial_year, section_id);
CREATE INDEX idx_ti_transaction ON tax_investments(transaction_id);

-- Salary Structures (multi-user aware)
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX idx_ss_fy ON salary_structures(user_id, financial_year);

-- Tax Regime Slabs (system lookup)
CREATE INDEX idx_trs_fy_regime ON tax_regime_slabs(financial_year, regime, slab_from);

-- ITR Documents (multi-user aware)
CREATE INDEX idx_itrd_fy ON itr_documents(user_id, financial_year);
```

### Query Performance Benchmarks (Target)

| Query | Target Time | Index Used |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Get all sections with limits | < 0.2ms | PRIMARY KEY scan (small table) |
| Get all investments for a user+FY | < 0.5ms | idx_ti_fy_section |
| Get section utilization (sum invested per section in FY, user) | < 1ms | idx_ti_section_fy (covering index) |
| Get salary info for a user+FY | < 0.5ms | idx_ss_fy (unique) |
| Get tax slabs for a regime in FY | < 0.5ms | idx_trs_fy_regime |
| Calculate tax payable (old regime — all deductions) | < 3ms | idx_ti_section_fy + client-side calculation |
| Calculate tax payable (new regime — no deductions) | < 0.5ms | idx_ss_fy + client-side calculation |
| Get ITR document checklist for user+FY | < 1ms | idx_itrd_fy |
| Add investment entry | < 2ms | PRIMARY KEY |
| Export section utilization for FY | < 2ms | idx_ti_section_fy |

## Suggested Enhancements

1. **Capital gains tracking** — Track short-term and long-term capital gains from investments (mutual funds, stocks, property) for tax reporting. Section 112A (LTCG on equity), Section 111A (STCG), and Section 54 (capital gains exemption on house sale reinvestment) are common Indian tax sections that users need to track for ITR filing.

2. **Form 16 parser** — Allow users to upload their Form 16 PDF. The system extracts salary components, TDS deducted, and employer PF details. This eliminates manual data entry of salary structure and prefills the tax regime comparison.

3. **Quarterly advance tax calculator** — For freelancers and professionals with tax liability over ₹10,000, calculate quarterly advance tax due dates (June 15, Sep 15, Dec 15, Mar 15) and amounts. Send reminders before each due date.

4. **TDS tracking integration** — Allow users to view TDS deducted across different income sources (salary, bank interest, rent, professional fees) by linking Form 26AS data. Track TDS credits against total tax liability.

5. **HRA exemption optimizer** — If the user's actual rent is lower than the HRA exemption they could claim, show a suggestion: "Increasing your rent by ₹X/month would save ₹Y in taxes." Bridges the gap between spending behavior and tax planning.

6. **Multi-year comparison** — Show a year-over-year comparison of section utilization, tax paid, and effective tax rate across the last 3-5 financial years. Users can see how their tax situation has changed as their income grew.
